# Dubiz Brain — Business Status Constitution v1

> **A conceptual constitution only.** No code, no schema, no migrations, no UI, no
> API, no signals, no Hero, no Home, no product behavior. It fixes the conceptual
> definition of **Business Status** — the first thing the owner sees, before any
> Insight, Judgment, or Recommendation — as derived from the Business Status Review
> and the Business Vital Signs Review.
>
> It sits beneath the Evidence & Reality Constitution, the Brain Internal
> Architecture, and the Belief Formation Constitution, and above any future
> implementation, which it does **not** prescribe.

---

## Part I — What Business Status Is

**1. Business Status is a Projection of the World Model.** It is a standing,
owner-facing view over the beliefs that describe the current condition of the
business.

**2. Business Status creates no new content.** It selects, projects, and arranges
existing beliefs; it produces no belief that is not already grounded in the World
Model.

**3. Business Status does not live inside the World Model.** It is a *view over* the
model, not an object within it.

**4. Business Status and Judgment are parallel Consumers of the World Model.** Both
read the model; Status *describes the condition*, Judgment *evaluates significance*.
Neither is downstream of the other.

**5. Business Status is a Pull Surface** — the ambient, always-available view the
owner consults.

**6. Insights are Push Objects** — judged, significance-bearing claims the Brain
surfaces.

**7. Status precedes Insight at the level of experience, not at the level of the
pipeline.** "Status first" is an ordering of what the owner sees, not a sequence of
processing; Status is a parallel projection, not a pipeline stage before Judgment.

---

## Part II — The Three Epistemic Moves

**8. Status = IS** — the descriptive condition.

**9. Insight = MATTERS** — the judged significance.

**10. Recommendation = DO** — the proposed action.

These are three distinct moves — describe → evaluate-significance → prescribe-action —
and they must remain three separate layers.

---

## Part III — The Atom: Condition

**11. Condition is the atom of Business Status.**

**12. A Condition is:** `{ Dimension Reading + Coverage / Certainty + Traceability }`.

**13. A Condition may contain:** Known, Unknown, Blind Spot, or Missing Coverage.

**14. Business Status must contain declared Unknowns.** What the system does not know
about the business's condition is first-class status content.

**15. Business Status must not present false completeness.** A status that hides its
gaps is the most dangerous lie, because it is the first thing the owner sees and
trusts.

---

## Part IV — Vital Signs

**16. A Vital Sign is not a Condition.**

**17. A Vital Sign is a Dimension** — a standing, structural axis of the business's
condition.

**18. A Condition is the current reading of a Vital Sign.**

**19. Vital Sign : Condition :: Question : Answer.** The Vital Sign is the standing
question; the Condition is the current answer.

**20. A Vital Sign must be:**
- **State-defining** — its value is part of what "the business's state" means,
- **Standing** — always relevant, not occasional,
- **Viability-linked** — its degradation can threaten the business's functioning.

**21. The Vital Sign test:** *"Would its absence leave the picture of the business
structurally incomplete?"* If yes, it is a Vital Sign; if its absence is harmless, it
is an incidental metric, not a Vital Sign.

---

## Part V — The Three Layers of Vital Signs and Their Authority

**22. Vital Signs exist in three nested layers:**
- **Universal** — every business has them.
- **Industry** — they belong to a class of business.
- **Business-Specific** — they belong to this particular business.

A business's set = Universal ∪ Industry ∪ Business-Specific, with Universal as the
floor.

**23. The three authorities, matching the three layers:**
- **Universal** ← the Constitution / first principles (what a business *is*).
- **Industry** ← an Industry Model (a structural model of the business type).
- **Business-Specific** ← the Business Owner + System Learning.

**24. Judgment is not authorized to choose Vital Signs.** Choosing the set is a
structural / constitutional / learned act, not a per-moment significance judgment.
Judgment evaluates the *condition* of a Vital Sign; it does not decide *that* a
dimension is vital.

**25. The Brain may learn Business-Specific Vital Signs** (descriptively, from
evidence about this business).

**26. The Brain is not authorized to create Universal or Prescriptive Vital Signs.**
It may not decide, by its own authority, what *ought* to matter for a business.

---

## Part VI — Unknown as a Vital Sign's Condition

**27. Unknown is a legal Condition of a Vital Sign.** A vital dimension whose value
the system cannot see is shown as Unknown, not omitted.

**28. A Vital Sign continues to exist even when its Condition = Unknown.** Because a
Vital Sign is a dimension, not a reading, the system can hold "this dimension is vital
**and** its value is unknown" — which is the most important honest statement a status
can make: a known-vital dimension the system is blind to.

---

## Part VII — The Structure of Business Status

**29. Business Status is a set of pairs:** `( Vital Sign, Current Condition )`.

The Vital-Sign set is a standing frame (from authority), held separately from the
World Model; the World Model supplies the content; a Condition is what results when a
Vital Sign is asked of the World Model. Business Status is the frame filled with
current conditions.

---

## Part VIII — The Honesty Contract

**30. Business Status must be:**
- **Traceable** — every Condition grounded in World Model beliefs (or in a declared gap),
- **Settlement-Honest** — never presenting a commitment as a settlement,
- **Blind-Spot-Honest** — declaring what it cannot see,
- **Coverage-Honest** — declaring where its perception is partial.

**31. Business Status must not contain:**
- **Insights** (judged significance),
- **Recommendations** (actions),
- **Judgments** (evaluations such as good/bad/healthy),
- **Narrative shaping** (silently curating the set to tell a story).

**32. Business Status must distinguish:** Committed, Physical Settled, Financial
Settled. These are orthogonal and may not be conflated.

**33. Status may not perform Settlement Collapse** — it may not present "issued,"
"recommended," or "delivered" as "paid." (Financial Settlement is currently
unobservable; status must say so.)

---

## Part IX — Scope and Non-Implementation

**34. This Constitution does not define UI.**

**35. This Constitution does not define Signals.**

**36. This Constitution does not define Hero.**

**37. This Constitution defines only the basic awareness layer** the owner sees
before any Insight or Recommendation.

**38. Non-Implementation Status.** This document does not implement product behavior,
does not implement the Brain, and does not define Home, Dashboard, Hero, or Signals.
It does not specify thresholds, formulas, or any concrete vital-sign list. It fixes
**only the conceptual constitution of Business Status** — what it is, its atom, its
honesty, and the nature and authority of Vital Signs — as the stable foundation that
future design and implementation must honor, but which it deliberately leaves
unspecified.
