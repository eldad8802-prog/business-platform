## Authority model

Normative authority is section-level, not file-level.

Committing the corpus does not make all of its contents uniformly normative.

Status is determined only by the applicable combination of:

* each section's explicit status;
* the Ratification Ledger;
* the Contracts Register;
* the CRE Register;
* applicable owner-approved Amendments.

Sections marked Proposed, Draft, Pending, Contract-Pending, awaiting ratification, or equivalent are not made Ratified by materialization or Git commit.

Sections recorded as Ratified retain their recorded status only insofar as that status is supported by the corpus's Ratification Ledger, Contracts Register, CRE Register, and applicable owner-approved Amendments. This MANIFEST does not independently ratify or reaffirm any section.
